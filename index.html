Menu:

Here are few of our house specialtyies. Click the "Place order" button for the complete menu.

Americano

Espresso diluted with hot water, yielding a strength similar to drip coffee, but with a distinct espresso flavor.

Latte

Espresso with a larger ratio of steamed milk topped with a thin layer of microfoam. Often paired with vanilla or caramel syrup.

Hot chocolate

Melted chocolate combined with steamed milk, and topped with whipped cream.

Chai latte

Spiced black tea concentrate mixed with steamed milk. with the option to make it a "Dirty Chai".
